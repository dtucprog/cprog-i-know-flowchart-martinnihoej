Making an Espresso

Input the desired coffee dose in grams.
Grind the coffee beans.
Put the ground coffee into the portafilter.
Tamp the coffee.
Insert the portafilter into the espresso machine.
Start the extraction.
Check if the extraction time is between 25 and 30 seconds.

If the extraction time is between 25 and 30 seconds, output "Espresso ready".

Otherwise, adjust the grind size and repeat the process.
